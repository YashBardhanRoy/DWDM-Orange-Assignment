# Experiment 4 — Association Rules with Apriori

**Input:** [`../adult.data`](../adult.data), after cleaning. Education was ignored; numeric attributes were discretized by equal-frequency binning. Minimum support was 30% and minimum confidence was 85%.

The report records 110 itemsets and 113 rules. Its top reported rule was: married-civ-spouse + US + White + Male ⇒ Husband (support 0.345, confidence 0.993, lift 2.45).
